Processing

Nothing runs until you press Start on the Clips page. After Start: Off prepares only the videos there at that moment; Prepare only also prepares videos added later, then each waits for your review; Full auto also marks every clip reviewed and finishes it without your review.
Clean: buildings as whole facades, simple masses for trees and cars, easy to read (recommended). Detailed: every depth block, closer to the picture but busy.
A failed job is tried again automatically this many times in total.

Folders

Your own folder: only you see its clips. Copy videos into it and they appear on the Clips page by themselves; change the folder there.

Background set · Claude

Who builds the walls, pillars, curtains, doors and furniture behind the people. Claude looks at the frames like an artist, uses the local measurements (camera, distances, people, outlines) as its ruler, includes every object you annotated, then checks its set over the film and corrects it. Local models build it from the measurements alone (free, but weaker on walls and pillars). With Claude, frames of each clip are sent to the Claude API (about $0.10–0.40 per clip with Sonnet). The key is stored only on this PC and is never shown again.

Used for the next Prepare. Finish reuses the set Prepare made unless the camera or your objects changed.
Each check shows Claude its set laid over the film and lets it fix positions; each round adds about the same cost again.

AI suggestions · DeepSeek

DeepSeek checks who is who and what the set really is (walls, cars, trees…), removes wrong parts and adds missed objects. Clip frames are sent to DeepSeek. The key is stored only on this PC and is never shown again.

The environment check needs a model that accepts images. Test tells you.

Delivery format · v2.1

Fixed by the client's v2.1 spec (docs\v21). One zip per clip, named after its package, e.g. the_hidden_blade-3.zip:

  • target/original.mp4: the source clip, unchanged
  • white_model/blendervid.mp4: the white model on a 1920×1080 frame (1080×1920 for a vertical source)
  • comparison/comparison.mp4: source on top, white model below, each 1920x1080 (1920x2160 in all)
  • model_3d/<package>.blend with model_3d/reconstruction/scene.json
  • meta/meta.json: paths, Blender version, frames, fps, duration
  • Both videos carry the source's own audio, copied unchanged.
