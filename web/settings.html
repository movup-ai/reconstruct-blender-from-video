Processing

How it runs
Automatic: after Start on the Clips page, every video without a finished zip runs to a checked v2.1 zip (people, camera, floor and objects), also videos added later.
A failed job is tried again automatically this many times in total.
m
The height assumed for a person when the video gives no better measure.
mm (35 mm equivalent)
Used when the lens cannot be measured from the picture.
Hold visible planted feet during fitting
Measure gravity, camera height and scale with MoGe-2 and Mask2Former
Person reconstruction
SAM 3 tracking → RTMPose masked joints → SAM 3D Body
SAM 3 keeps mask identities through overlaps. RTMPose measures joints inside each person's mask.
SAM 3D Body uses tracked masks and camera intrinsics for metric pose, scale and depth. Use interval 1 for every observed frame, or 2 for faster inference.
A clip whose zip was made with other settings shows out of date; Start makes it again.

Delivery format · v2.1

Fixed by the client's v2.1 spec. One zip per clip, named after its package, e.g. the_hidden_blade-3.zip, checked automatically before the job counts as done:

  • target/original.mp4: the source clip, byte for byte
  • white_model/blendervid.mp4: the white model on a 1920×1080 frame (1080×1920 for a vertical source)
  • comparison/comparison.mp4: the source and the white model, each 1080P: 1920×2160 stacked (2160×1920 side by side for a vertical source)
  • model_3d/<package>.blend with model_3d/reconstruction/scene.json
  • meta/meta.json: paths, Blender version, frames, fps, duration
  • Both videos carry the source's own audio, copied unchanged.
