Processing

Off: nothing starts by itself. Prepare only: every clip is prepared, then waits for you. Full auto: prepared, checked by DeepSeek (if a key is saved), confirmed and rendered.
3 suits a PC with an NVIDIA card (RTX 4070, 12 GB). Use 1 on a PC without one, where the models run on the processor. Lower it if the PC gets slow or the card runs out of memory.
Clean: buildings as whole facades, simple masses for trees and cars, easy to read (recommended). Detailed: every depth block, closer to the picture but busy.
A failed job is tried again automatically this many times in total.
