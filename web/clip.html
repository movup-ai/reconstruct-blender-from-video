1Who is who?

Each card is one person the models followed. Click their role. The colour follows the role (主角色 red, 第二角色 blue, 第三角色 green, 第四角色 yellow). Use Not a person for false detections, such as a driver inside a car or a reflection. If the same character appears in two shots, give both cards the same role. ✦ marks DeepSeek's suggestion.

2How does the camera move?

Already filled in from what the camera solver measured. Change it if it's wrong.

3Set and objects

The set is made of walls and blocks fitted to the depth model, coloured with the video's own colours (L2). In the picture, the left side is the empty background and the right side is what it became. Add objects that people touch or that hide them.

4Objects in the set

Everything a viewer should recognise: doors, shelves, counters, tables, cars, trees… Each one is built as a simple model of its type, like the client sample. Drag on a picture to add an object, click a box to select it, Delete removes it. With a DeepSeek key saved, this list is filled in automatically.
