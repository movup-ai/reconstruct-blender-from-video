1Who is who?

Give each person a role. Mark statues, pictures and false detections Not a person, then press Re-check (about a minute). The same character in two shots gets the same role.

2Fix joints (every 8th frame)

Starts on the frames where the 3D person moved suddenly (orange line; F = next). Drag a dot to fix it, Enter = frame OK. Your fixes are kept when you press any button below.

3Objects in the set

Drag a box on the picture around anything a viewer should recognise (door, shelf, table, tree…) and pick its type. Click a box to select it, Delete removes it. Re-check builds them into the set.
