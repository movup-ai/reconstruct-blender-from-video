Help

Blender Agent turns film clips into white-model packages in the client's v2.1 format, automatically. Everything runs on this PC.

The workflow

  1. Copy the videos into the source folder (the link at the top of the Clips page opens it). Each video appears as a card by itself; the ✕ on a card takes it off the list (the file stays where it is, and Removed from the list below the cards puts it back). Give the videos their delivery names once with Name videos…: the client requires each zip to carry one. Type the film's English title and they get title-1, title-2… in file-name order.
  2. Start on the Clips page. Nothing runs until you press it, also after restarting the app. Every video without a finished zip (new, failed, cancelled or out of date) then runs Stage 1 · Movement by itself, one straight run with no review step:
    • people and their joints (SAM 3 tracking, RTMPose, SAM 3D Body), the camera with its zoom, the metric scale and a grey floor;
    • a Blender scene with the sample character, the white-model video and the comparison video;
    • the v2.1 zip in SUBMIT, checked against the delivery spec before the clip counts as done.
    Videos added while your queue runs join it by themselves. The Clips page shows each video's progress and how long it took.
  3. Pause, Resume, Cancel: Pause processing stops active jobs and keeps completed caches; Resume processing (or Start) restarts the interrupted run where the caches allow, in its old place in the queue. Cancel pipeline stops your running and waiting jobs without retries; completed files stay. Each active clip also has its own Cancel. ✕ on a card skips a video: it stays on the list, greyed, and Start leaves it out (the file and any finished zip are kept). Include brings it back.
  4. Deliver: download each zip with its Download button (on the card, the clip page or the Submissions page). It is saved under its delivery name (e.g. the_hidden_blade-3.zip), which the client's checker requires. Do not send the zips straight from the SUBMIT folder: those are named after the clip. Check packages on the Submissions page checks every zip again.

Stage 2 · Background is coming later: it will place objects and the set from the depth Stage 1 already saved, then render and pack the zip again.

Questions

A clip says "no delivery name"

The client's delivery rules (v2.1) require every zip to be named after the film's English title and a number, for example the_hidden_blade-3. A video without one is given a made-up name (clip-1, clip-2…) when it is queued, so it is never lost after hours of work; it is marked auto. To use the film's title, press Name videos… on the Clips page and type it: made-up names are replaced (and never used again) and the zip is made again; real names never change.

A clip says "out of date"

Its zip was made from an older video, delivery name or Stage 1 settings. Start (or Re-run on the clip) makes it again.

When can I add videos?

Any time, even while others are being processed. Copy them into the source folder: within a few seconds they appear on the Clips page, and while the queue runs they are queued by themselves. Otherwise press Start.

Where are the results?

Packages and zips are in the SUBMIT folder (Submissions page → Open SUBMIT folder). Processing results, previews and logs are in the Work folder (Settings).

What do the colours mean?

People use the colour card: red, blue, green, yellow, then cyan, magenta… The floor is grey.

A clip shows "failed"

Open it: the red box says what went wrong and what to do, and the technical log is below. Retry failed on the Clips page, or Start, tries all failed clips again.

Can I close the window?

Yes. Unfinished jobs wait in the queue; press Start after you open Blender Agent again. If it ever crashes, the launcher restarts it after 5 seconds, also paused.

Is anything sent over the internet?

No. The models run on this PC's NVIDIA card.
