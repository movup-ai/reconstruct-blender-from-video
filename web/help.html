Help

Blender Agent turns film clips into white-model (白模) packages in the client's v2.1 format. Everything runs on this PC.

The workflow

  1. Choose a source folder: step 1 on the Clips page, Choose folder…. Any folder on this PC; the videos stay there. Type an English title once: videos without a v2.1 package name get title-1, title-2… in file-name order. Choosing a folder does not start anything.
  2. Start: step 2. Nothing runs until you press Start, also after restarting the app. The local models then find people, joints, lens, camera, cuts, ground and set; the Clips page shows each video's step and progress. About 3–5 minutes per video on this PC. Pause lets the running video finish and starts nothing new.
  3. Review each clip marked ready for review (step 3, Review next clip):
    • 1 Who is who: give each person a role. A card saying Not a person? looks like a statue, a picture or a false detection: one click, then Re-check.
    • 2 Joints: the editor opens on the frames where the 3D person moved suddenly. Drag wrong dots into place.
    • 3 Objects: drag a box around each thing a viewer should recognise (door, shelf, tree…).
    Then press ✓ Mark reviewed (finish later, together with others) or ▶ Finish now. Camera, set, held objects and the description are filled in automatically; change them under More only if they are wrong.
  4. Finish: Finish all reviewed on the Clips page (or Finish now on a clip) runs the final fit, set, Blender, videos and the v2.1 zip into SUBMIT. A clip with no changes since its last Finish is skipped, so nothing renders twice.
  5. Deliver: on the Submissions page, press Check packages. Then download each zip with its Zip button: it is saved under its package name (e.g. the_hidden_blade-3.zip), which the client's checker requires. Do not send the zips straight from the SUBMIT folder: those are named after the clip. For a whole batch with the manifest, run pipeline\deliver.py; it writes the correctly named zips to DELIVERY\UPLOAD.

Settings → Autopilot Full auto does steps 3–4 by itself after Start, without your review. With a DeepSeek key saved, DeepSeek checks the people and the set first.

Questions

A clip says "no package name"

It cannot be finished without a v2.1 package name. On the Clips page, press Choose folder…, choose the same folder again and type its English title: only videos without a name get one, existing names never change.

A clip says "changed since Finish"

Its roles, joints, objects or description changed after its last Finish, so its package is out of date. Finish all reviewed makes it again.

When can I add videos?

Any time, even while others are being prepared. Copy them into the source folder, click look for new videos in step 1 on the Clips page, then press Start.

Where are the results?

Packages and zips are in the SUBMIT folder (Submissions page → Open SUBMIT folder). Preparation results, previews and logs are in the Work folder (Settings).

What do the colours mean?

People: 主角色 red, 第二角色 blue, 第三角色 green, 第四角色 yellow, other people purple. The set is light grey like the client samples; More → Set can switch it to the video's own colours. The colours in More → Held objects (touched or held orange, far interactive pink, special purple, foreground brown, light source white) are for objects people hold or touch, not for the objects of step 3.

A clip shows "failed"

Open it: the red box says what went wrong and what to do. Retry failed in step 2 on the Clips page tries all failed clips again.

How do I get recognisable objects (doors, shelves, cars…)?

On a clip page, step 3 Objects: drag a box around an object on the background picture, pick its type (door, cabinet, shelf, counter, table, chair, car, tree, lamp post…), whether it stands on the floor or hangs on a wall, and which way it faces. Save keeps your boxes; Re-check builds them into the set, each as a simple model of its type at the right size. With a DeepSeek key saved, the list is filled in for you and you only correct it.

The set looks wrong

On the clip page, open More → Set and look at the set picture (green = ground, blue = sky, red lines = where walls stand). Fix people's joints if needed and press Re-check, or let DeepSeek check the set (Settings → AI suggestions).

Can I close the window?

Yes. Unfinished jobs wait in the queue; press Start after you open Blender Agent again. If it ever crashes, the launcher restarts it after 5 seconds, also paused.

Is anything sent over the internet?

Only when DeepSeek AI suggestions are on: then frames of the clips go to DeepSeek. Everything else stays on this PC.
