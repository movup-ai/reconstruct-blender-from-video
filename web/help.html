Help

Blender Agent turns film clips into white-model (白模) submissions like the client sample 11月2日试稿. Everything runs on this PC.

The workflow

  1. Choose source folder on the Clips page: any folder on this PC. The videos stay there; joints, work files and packages go to the project's output, Work_auto and SUBMIT folders. Type an English title once: videos without a v2.1 package name get title-1, title-2… in file-name order. Choosing a folder does not start anything. (You can also drag videos in, or copy them into the folder and press Rescan.)
  2. Start: nothing runs until you press Start on the Clips page (also after restarting the app). Then the local models find people, joints, lens, camera, cuts, ground and set. The Clips page shows each video's step and progress. About 4–5 minutes per clip on this PC.
  3. Review a clip marked ready for review: 1 Who is who (a card saying Not a person? looks like a statue, picture or false detection: one click, then Re-check), 2 Joints (the editor opens on the flagged frames first), 3 Objects (drag boxes on the background: door, jar, tree…). Then press ✓ Mark reviewed (finish later, with others) or ▶ Finish now. Camera, set, held objects and the description are filled in automatically; change them under More only if they are wrong.
  4. Finish: Finish all reviewed on the Clips page (or Finish on a clip) runs the final fit, set, Blender, videos and the v2.1 zip into SUBMIT. A clip whose roles, joints, objects and notes have not changed since its last Finish is skipped, so nothing renders twice.
  5. Deliver: on the Submissions page, press Check all against the sample, then send the zips.

Settings → Autopilot Full auto does steps 2–4 by itself after Start, without your review. With a DeepSeek key saved, DeepSeek checks the people and the set first.

Questions

When can I add videos?

Any time, even while clips are rendering. Copy them into the source folder and click look for new videos on the Clips page, then press Start.

Where are the results?

Packages and zips are in the SUBMIT folder (Submissions page → Open SUBMIT folder). Previews and logs are in the Work folder.

What do the colours mean?

主角色 red, 第二角色 blue, 第三角色 green, 第四角色 yellow, other people purple. Objects: touched/held orange, far interactive pink, special purple, foreground brown, light white. The set uses the video's own colours (L2 spec).

A clip shows "failed"

Open it: the red box says what went wrong and what to do. Retry failed on the Clips page tries all failed clips again.

How do I get recognisable objects (doors, shelves, cars…) like the client sample?

On a clip page, section 4 Objects in the set: drag a box around an object on the background picture, pick its type (door, cabinet, shelf, counter, table, chair, car, tree, lamp post…), say whether it stands on the floor or hangs on a wall, and which way it faces. Press Save objects & rebuild the set. Each object is built as a simple model of its type, placed where it touches the floor, at the right size. With a DeepSeek key saved, the list is filled in for you and you only correct it.

The set looks wrong

On the clip page, look at the set picture (green = ground, blue = sky, red lines = where walls stand). Fix people's joints if needed and press Re-check, or let DeepSeek check the set (Settings → AI suggestions).

Can I close the window?

Yes. Unfinished jobs continue the next time you start Blender Agent. If it ever crashes, the launcher restarts it after 5 seconds.

Is anything sent over the internet?

Only when DeepSeek AI suggestions are on: then frames of the clips go to DeepSeek. Everything else stays on this PC.
