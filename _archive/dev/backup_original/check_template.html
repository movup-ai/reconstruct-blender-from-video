How to check

Red line = person's RIGHT side, pink line = LEFT side.

Drag a dot to fix it. Wheel = zoom. Drag empty space = move the view.

Right-click a dot (or H) = hidden / not visible.

Click a person's dot to select them. S swaps left/right, D deletes a wrong detection, A adds a missed person.

Enter = frame is OK, go to next. ← → = change frame.

When all frames are green, press Save (Ctrl+S). In the app it is saved for you; opened on its own, keep checked.json next to check.html.
